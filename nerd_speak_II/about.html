About

My name is Theo Osborn and I am the creator of the programming langauge Nerd_speak. Nerd_speak is a programming language designed to look like Dungeons and Dragons (DnD) adventures when it is typed out. It has come about as my Senior Thesis project for Arcadia University. It combines two things that I greatly enjoy, programming and DnD. Nerd_speak is greatly inspired by other esoteric programming langauges such as the Shakespeare programming langauge and Chef.
